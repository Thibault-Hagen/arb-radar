    Before you place anything

    • Prices move between scans. Check both sides on the venue itself, place the thinner side first, and skip the trade if either price has changed.
    • Settlement rules can differ. Read how each venue handles ties, overtime and postponed games before you rely on a hedge.
    • Sportsbooks require you to be 21 or older in Illinois and most other states, and they often limit accounts that only take arbs.
    • Your money is tied up until the game settles, and moving cash between venues can take days.
